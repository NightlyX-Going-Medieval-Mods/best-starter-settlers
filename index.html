How it works

Every option below comes from the game files (data/*.csv, extracted from StreamingAssets).

Game rules (checked in the decompiled game code)

  • Points: a settler costs trait costs + Σ (skill level − level from traits). The team limit is 90 × settlers, shared, with no per-settler cap. Levels from traits are free, and each extra level costs 1 point.
  • Negative modifiers cost points: levels can't go below 0, so a trait giving Art −3 costs 3 points.
  • Skill levels: 0 to 50.
  • Interests (passions) can't be bought. They come only from backgrounds, pseudonym and perks. Stacking: the same level twice stays the same, and different levels combine as clamp(a + b − 3, 1, 5). So Eager + Passionate = Passionate, and Eager + Unwilling = Indifferent.
  • Perks: up to 10 in Advanced Customization, at any age. Age brackets only affect random settlers. Conflicting perks can't be combined.
  • Body type: backgrounds and pseudonyms tagged Male/Female are only for that body type (Monk, Nun, The Spear Maiden, The Paramour). Perks aren't restricted in the editor.
  • Age: doesn't affect perks or points. Old-age death only starts at 66.

Value

Each settler has a priority (how much you care) and a target level per skill. Levels up to the target are worth priority each. Levels above it, up to the max level, are worth only 20% of that. Interests add interest weight × priority × bonus: Passionate +2 · Eager +1 · Indifferent 0 · Unwilling −1 · Resentful −2 · Incapable −3. In the game files Passionate gives ×4 skill gain and Eager ×2.5.

Solo: spread the targets (a generalist with 6–10 in the survival skills). Team: give each settler a deep main skill plus a few low-priority secondaries, so no points go unspent.

Optimizer

For several "prices per point", every settler picks the backgrounds/pseudonym/perks that are worth more than buying those levels directly. The leftover pool then goes to skill levels across the whole team, cheapest gain first. The best team overall is kept. Perk sets respect conflicts, the perk limit and your cap on point-refund perks, and are then refined by trying to add, drop or swap single perks. Click an alternative to swap a settler's traits: skill points are re-spent automatically.

Not scored (judge these yourself)

  • Perk effects other than skills and interests: movement speed, XP gain, mood rules, chemistry with other perks. They're shown in each perk's details.
  • Ascetic (−13) ignores every bed, bedroom and comfort mood, good or bad, and dislikes rooms that are "too impressive". It's often fine in a rough early colony.
